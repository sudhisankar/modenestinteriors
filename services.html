What We Offer

Our Services

From creative concepts to complete execution, Modnest Interiors provides integrated interior design and fit-out solutions tailored to each project's requirements.

Our Capabilities

Complete Design & Fit-Out Services

01 / Interior Design

Interior Design

Comprehensive interior design solutions that combine aesthetics with functionality. We develop designs tailored to your lifestyle, preferences, and spatial requirements — creating environments that are visually compelling and practically sound. From concept development through to detailed design drawings and material selections.

02 / Space Planning

Space Planning

Strategic space planning to optimise layout, flow, and functionality for every type of environment. We analyse your space, understand your operational requirements, and design layouts that maximise efficiency, comfort, and visual appeal — ensuring every square metre is used purposefully.

03 / Visualisation

3D Visualization & Rendering

Photorealistic 3D visualisations and renderings that allow you to experience your space before a single element is installed. Our detailed renderings provide a clear and accurate representation of the proposed design — enabling informed decisions on layouts, finishes, colours, and furnishings.

04 / Concepts

Creative Spatial Concepts

Innovative and imaginative spatial concepts that translate your vision into a distinctive design direction. We explore creative possibilities, propose unique spatial solutions, and develop concept presentations that capture the character and atmosphere of your intended space.

05 / Development

Design Development

Detailed design development that takes approved concepts through to fully coordinated design documentation. We refine every element — from furniture layouts and material specifications to lighting and joinery details — ensuring a complete and buildable design package.

06 / Fit-Out

Interior Fit-Out Works

Complete interior fit-out execution with skilled craftsmanship and close attention to every finishing detail. From partitioning and ceiling works to flooring, joinery, and specialist finishes — we manage the full fit-out process with quality and precision, ensuring the design is faithfully realised.

07 / Decoration

Interior Decoration

Curated interior decoration services that bring personality, warmth, and character to a completed space. We select and source furnishings, lighting, soft furnishings, artwork, and accessories that complement the overall design and reflect the client's individual taste and lifestyle.

08 / Materials

Material & Finish Selection

Expert guidance on material and finish selections to ensure quality, coherence, and appropriateness for each environment. We advise on flooring, wall finishes, surface materials, hardware, and paint — ensuring all selections align with the design intent and project budget.

09 / Coordination

Design Coordination

Professional coordination between design disciplines, consultants, contractors, and suppliers to ensure a coherent and well-executed outcome. We manage design interfaces, resolve conflicts, and maintain design integrity from concept through to completion.

10 / Presentation

Project Presentation

Professional design presentations that clearly communicate the design vision, rationale, and proposed solutions. We prepare comprehensive presentations suitable for client review, stakeholder approval, or regulatory submission — ensuring all parties are aligned and informed at every stage.

11 / Transformation

Complete Space Transformation

End-to-end project delivery that covers the full journey from initial concept development through design, fit-out, decoration, and final handover. Our complete space transformation service provides a single point of responsibility for the entire project — ensuring a seamless, well-managed process and a beautifully finished result that meets your vision, budget, and programme.

Our Specialisations

Spaces We Transform

We work across three key sectors, delivering tailored design and fit-out solutions for every type of environment.

Residential

We design and fit-out private residences across the UAE — from villas and apartments to luxury homes and private spaces. Our residential projects focus on creating environments that are highly personal, functional, and reflective of the owner's lifestyle and taste.


Villas · Apartments · Private Residences · Luxury Homes

Commercial

Professional interior design and fit-out for commercial environments — creating spaces that align with brand identity, support operational requirements, and create a positive impression for clients, customers, and staff.


Offices · Retail Spaces · Showrooms · Business Premises

Hospitality

Distinctive interior design and fit-out for hospitality venues — creating memorable guest experiences through carefully considered atmospheres, materials, and spatial design. We understand the importance of brand, ambience, and operational efficiency in hospitality environments.


Hotels · Restaurants · Cafés · Hospitality Venues
