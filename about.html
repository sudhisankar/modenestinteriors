Our Journey in Design & Innovation

Modnest Interiors is a UAE-based interior design and fit-out company creating functional, elegant, and inspiring spaces for residential, commercial, and hospitality projects. Originally established in 2023 as an engineering consultancy, we provided interior design concepts, visualization, spatial concepts, design development, and project presentations.

Building on this foundation, the company evolved under the name Modnest Interiors, specialising in interior design, fit-out works, decoration, and complete space transformation. Operating under the current brand since 2026, we are backed by 14+ years of professional experience in engineering, design, construction, planning, and project execution.

Our experience combines creative design, technical expertise, quality workmanship, attention to detail, and professional project management to deliver practical and aesthetically appealing spaces, while maintaining the highest standards of quality, innovation, integrity, and client satisfaction.
